Figure 1. Methodology pipeline. Four filters (≤ 80 km from FDNPP, dose > 0.10 µSv/h, ≥ 4 measurement dates, single survey type) reduce 14.5 M JAEA EMDB records to 138,768 mesh cells. Nine IQR-scaled temporal features per cell train a hexagonal Self-Organizing Map across two grid sizes (5×5 and 6×6), five random seeds, and 2,000 iterations per run. Model selection used quantization error, topographic error, and Adjusted Rand Index, returning the 5×5 grid with seed 123 (QE = 1.11, TE = 0.33, ARI = 0.853). Ward's D2 hierarchical clustering on the 25 codebook vectors gave six contamination decay regimes, with k = 6 chosen on silhouette and interpretability.
