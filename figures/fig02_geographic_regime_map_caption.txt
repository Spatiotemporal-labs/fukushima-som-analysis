Figure 2. Geographic distribution of contamination decay regimes identified by Self-Organizing Map clustering across 138,768 mesh cells in the Fukushima region. Each point represents a 250-m grid cell colored by its assigned regime. The black triangle marks the Fukushima Daiichi Nuclear Power Station (FDNPP), and the dashed circle the initial 20 km evacuation radius. Regimes were determined by hierarchical clustering (Ward's D2, k=4) of a 5x5 hexagonal SOM trained on 8 temporal features.
