Figure S5. Three methods for selecting the number of clusters (k). Left: average silhouette width (statistical optimum at k=3). Center: within-cluster sum of squares (elbow method). Right: gap statistic with bootstrap SE bars (B=100; optimum at k=1). The red dashed line marks the selected k=4, chosen as the smallest k that resolves the near-field high-dose regime at negligible silhouette cost (see Results).
